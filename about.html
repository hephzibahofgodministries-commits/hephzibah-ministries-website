WHO WE ARE

About Hephzibah of God Ministries

A ministry committed to lifting up God's standard through the Word of God in every aspect of life.

Our Vision

To raise a holy, prayerful, spiritually mature and evangelistically committed people who will know God, manifest the character of Christ and become instruments for the advancement of God's Kingdom.

Our Mission

The mission of the Ministry shall include:

  1. To preach the Gospel of Jesus Christ to all people.
  2. To lead sinners to genuine repentance and salvation.
  3. To teach believers the whole counsel of God.
  4. To promote holiness and righteousness.
  5. To cultivate a strong and consistent prayer life among believers.
  6. To encourage spiritual growth and Christian maturity.
  7. To equip believers for effective Christian service.
  8. To raise godly leaders.
  9. To engage in evangelism, missions and church planting.
  10. To minister to families, children, youths, women and men.
  11. To provide biblical teaching concerning Christian marriage and family life.
  12. To encourage compassion and responsible Christian service to humanity.
  13. To confront ungodliness and the works of darkness through biblical truth, prayer and righteous living.
  14. To prepare believers for the Second Coming of Jesus Christ.
  15. To make disciples who will make disciples.

Our Mandate

To lift up God's standard in the Word of God, which is the Bible, in all aspects of our lives to the whole world.

Our Statement of Faith

Hephzibah of God Ministries believes:

  1. In one God, eternally existing in three Persons: Father, Son and Holy Spirit.
  2. That the Bible is the inspired Word of God and the final authority for Christian faith and conduct.
  3. In the deity of Jesus Christ.
  4. In the virgin birth of Jesus Christ.
  5. In the sinless life of Jesus Christ.
  6. In the death of Jesus Christ for the sins of humanity.
  7. In the bodily resurrection of Jesus Christ.
  8. In the ascension of Jesus Christ into heaven.
  9. In the present ministry of the Holy Spirit.
  10. In the necessity of repentance and the new birth.
  11. In salvation by grace through faith in Jesus Christ.
  12. In sanctification and holy Christian living.
  13. In water baptism.
  14. In the baptism and operation of the Holy Spirit according to Scripture.
  15. In the Lord's Supper.
  16. In divine healing according to the sovereignty and power of God.
  17. In the reality of angels and Satan and the spiritual conflict described in Scripture.
  18. In the resurrection of the dead.
  19. In the final judgment.
  20. In the eternal reward of the righteous and eternal punishment of the unrepentant.
  21. In the Second Coming of Jesus Christ.
  22. In the Great Commission of the Church.
